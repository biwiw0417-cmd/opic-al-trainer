2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Actually, looking back, technology has completely revolutionized the way we look for homes. It’s a night-and-day contrast compared to how things were maybe ten or fifteen years ago.

In the past, people had to **rely heavily on** newspaper ads or physically visit a **reputable agent** just to get a glimpse of what was available. You know, it involved a lot of actual legwork. I remember my parents walking around neighborhoods for hours just to find 'For Sale' signs. It was incredibly time-consuming and honestly, quite exhausting.

But these days, the whole process has shifted online. We can **sift through** thousands of listings **at the touch of a button** using various apps. The thing is, these platforms provide so much detail now. You can take a **virtual tour** or look at high-resolution photos before even setting foot in the house. This really helps to **narrow down** the options without wasting any time.

Personally, I find this trend to be a total game-changer. It’s so much more efficient because you can filter by price, location, and even neighborhood vibes. I can't imagine going back to the old way of doing things. Overall, technology has made house hunting much more transparent and accessible for everyone.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(기술로 인한 주택 시장의 혁명적 변화) → MP(과거의 아날로그 방식 설명) → 디테일 확장(현재의 디지털 방식과 편리함) → 감정/생각(기술이 준 효율성에 대한 긍정적 평가) → 마무리(과거로 돌아갈 수 없음을 강조)

✨ 과거 시제(had to rely, involved, remember walking)와 현재 시제(has shifted, can sift)를 명확하게 교차 사용하여 AL 수준의 시제 통제력을 보여줍니다.
✨ ‘The thing is’, ‘Actually’, ‘You know’와 같은 디스코스 마커를 적재적소에 배치하여 답변의 흐름이 매우 자연스럽습니다.
