2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Well, when I think about the public transit system in Korea, it has undergone a total transformation over the last few decades. Honestly, looking back, things were quite different from how they are now.

The main point is that we have moved from a fragmented system to a highly integrated network. Back in the day, I remember my parents were heavily reliant on physical maps or just asking around to maneuver through the city. There were no smart apps, so if you missed a bus, you just had to wait indefinitely. It was quite a hassle, you know?

But nowadays, we have this top-notch infrastructure that is incredibly efficient. Everything is digital now. We use contactless payment with our smartphones or credit cards, and we get real-time updates on exactly when the next bus or train will arrive. This has been a vast improvement for anyone who has to commute to work daily.

What makes the current system truly remarkable is how the subway and bus systems are linked. Even if I have to transfer multiple times, the transitions are so smooth that I can reach my destination without any stress. It really makes life in a crowded city like Seoul much more bearable. Compared to how things used to be, it is like night and day.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(교통 체계의 총체적 변화 언급) → MP(통합 네트워크로의 발전) → 과거 디테일(종이 지도 의존, 대기 시간 불확실) → 현재 디테일(최첨단 인프라, 비접촉 결제, 실시간 정보) → 감정 및 마무리(스트레스 없는 이동, 과거와의 극명한 대조)

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 과거 시제(were, missed, had to wait)와 현재 시제(have, use, get)를 넘나들며 시제 대조를 명확하게 구현했습니다.
✨ 'it is like night and day', 'undergone a total transformation' 같은 관용적 표현과 구어체 필러(honestly, you know)를 자연스럽게 섞었습니다.
✨ 단순한 나열이 아니라, 기술적 진보(digital, real-time)가 일상에 미친 영향(without any stress)을 연결하여 디테일을 확장했습니다.
