2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Well, speaking of concerts, one live performance that really stands out in my memory happened last summer when I went to an outdoor music festival with my close friends.

Honestly, it was super tough to snag a ticket because the event sold out almost instantly. Fortunately, after refreshing the browser page like crazy, we managed to secure our entry. When we arrived at the venue, the energy was unbelievable. The moment the main band walked onto the stage, the whole crowd erupted, creating an electrifying atmosphere that gave me chills from head to toe.

During their hit song, everybody started to sing at the top of their lungs. I completely lost myself in the music and forgot about all my work stress. In contrast to listening to recorded tracks at home on my phone, experiencing the live sound with thousands of screaming fans was on a whole different level.

After their final song, the audience kept chanting until the band came back for an incredible encore performance. The concert ended with deafening applause that echoed long after the lights came on.

Looking back, it was genuinely one of the most exciting nights of my life. I still get excited whenever I hear that band on the radio.
