Well, Eva, talking about my very first solo trip brings back so many vivid memories. Looking back, it was right after graduating from college when I decided to take the plunge and backpack across Jeju Island completely on my own.

To be honest, the main point is that it was a huge leap of faith for me. Up until that point, I had always traveled under the wing of my parents, so stepping out of my comfort zone was downright terrifying. When I arrived, the weather was gloomy and I felt totally overwhelmed by the sheer thought of navigating everything solo.

Actually, the real challenge started on day two. I got lost while hiking along the coastal trail because my phone battery died. Without GPS, I had to think on my feet and rely on physical trail markers and ask local villagers for directions. The thing is, what started as a panic turned into something truly magical. The locals were so warm-hearted, and finding my way back gave me an unbelievable sense of accomplishment.

That whole experience served as a rite of passage for me. It completely reshaped my perspective and instilled confidence that I could handle any unpredictable challenge life throws at me.

So yeah, that first solo trip wasn't just a vacation; it truly fostered my independence and remains an unforgettable milestone in my life.
