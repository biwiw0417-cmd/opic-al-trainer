2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

You know, when I think about how things have changed, my neighborhood park immediately comes to mind. Back in the day, say, ten years ago, it was really just a simple patch of dirt with a couple of rusted benches. People rarely went there except for a quick morning jog, and honestly, the place felt kind of neglected and desolate.

Looking back, the main point is that the local government completely revamped the area a few years ago, turning it into a smart, eco-friendly community hub. Nowadays, if you walk through the park, you'll notice sleek paved walking trails, smart fitness equipment, and lush green landscaping that really breathes life into the neighborhood.

The thing is, the atmosphere has undergone a night-and-day contrast. It used to be eerily quiet and dark at night, but now it's constantly bustling with families, seniors, and young professionals enjoying their evenings under bright LED lights.

I actually feel so thrilled about this transformation, especially since it has dramatically improved our daily quality of life. Back then, I barely spent any time outdoors, but these days, I find myself heading over there almost every single evening to unwind after work.

All in all, seeing how much this space has evolved makes me appreciate modern urban development so much more. It just goes to show how thoughtful planning can completely reshape our living environment for the better.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(동네 공원의 변화 소개) → MP(지자체의 리모델링으로 에코 허브로 변모) → 디테일(과거의 황량함 vs 현재의 산책로와 스마트 시설, 분위기의 극적 변화) → 감정/생각(삶의 질 향상과 퇴근 후 휴식 공간으로서의 만족감) → 마무리(도시 계획의 긍정적 영향 요약)

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 과거 시제('was really just', 'felt kind of neglected')와 현재 시제('is constantly bustling', 'find myself heading')를 명확히 대조하여 시간 흐름에 따른 변화를 생생하게 전달했습니다.
✨ 'Back in the day', 'Looking back', 'The thing is', 'All in all' 같은 자연스러운 디스코스 마커를 사용하여 원어민스러운 구어체 흐름을 살렸습니다.
✨ 단순히 장소가 바뀌었다는 사실에 그치지 않고, '삶의 질 향상', '퇴근 후 휴식' 등 개인적 감정과 일상의 변화를 구체적으로 연결하여 답변의 깊이를 더했습니다.
