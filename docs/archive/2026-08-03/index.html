2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

To be honest, my musical tastes have evolved quite a bit over the years. Back in my high school days, I used to blast music through my headphones whenever I was studying or commuting. Back then, I had a soft spot for energetic rock and hip-hop because those intense beats helped me vent stress. I loved to sing along to every single chorus without caring what anyone thought.

However, as I started my corporate career, my daily routine became much more fast-paced and demanding. Naturally, my listening habits shifted dramatically. These days, I rarely listen to loud tracks. Instead, I prefer listening to soothing acoustic or jazz pieces while working or relaxing at home. The main reason is that soft melodies really help soothe my nerves after a long, exhausting day at work.

That being said, I still play songs with an upbeat rhythm when I need a quick boost during my workouts. What's truly fascinating is how certain old tracks can instantly evoke memories of my youth and make me feel nostalgic. Looking back, music has always been an essential part of my life, but its role has transformed from letting off steam to finding inner tranquility.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론: 음악 취향의 전반적 변화 언급 → MP(과거): 학창 시절 강렬한 음악을 즐겨 듣던 습관 → 디테일(현재): 직장 생활 후 신경을 안정시키는 잔잔한 음악 선호 → 감정/생각: 운동 및 추억 회상 시 음악의 다양한 역할 → 마무리: 스트레스 해소에서 마음의 평화 찾기로 전환된 음악의 의의 정리

✨ 단순히 '좋아한다'에 그치지 않고 soothe my nerves, evoke memories, vent stress 등 정서적 상태와 음악의 효과를 다채로운 덩어리 어휘로 구체화했습니다.
