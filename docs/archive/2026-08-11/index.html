You indicated in the survey that you like to go on trips. Can you walk me through your usual step-by-step process when preparing for a trip? Tell me everything you do from start to finish before leaving.

2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Preparing for a trip is honestly half the fun for me, but it definitely requires a lot of planning.

In the past, I used to just wing it and pack my bags at the last minute. But these days, I have a systematic routine. First off, a few weeks before leaving, I map out an itinerary to decide where I want to go and what I want to see each day.

Once the plan is set, I start to hunt for deals on flights and accommodations online. Finding good rates gives me a huge sense of satisfaction. As soon as I find reasonable prices, I lock in a booking right away so prices don't jump up later.

On the day before departure, I focus on getting my gear together. I try not to overpack, so I strictly pack the essentials like chargers, basic toiletries, and proper clothing.

Most of the time, the whole preparation process is smooth sailing. But actually, on my last trip to Osaka, I ran into a minor hiccup. I completely forgot to double-check my passport's expiration date until the night before. Thankfully, I had just enough time left on it, but looking back, it was a heart-stopping moment.

Overall, thorough preparation makes my actual travel experience so much more stress-free and enjoyable.

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 과거 시제 서사 및 시제 대조: 'In the past, I used to...'로 과거 습관을 언급한 뒤 현재 루틴으로 전환하고, 'on my last trip to Osaka, I ran into...' 구체적 과거 시점으로 매끄럽게 시제를 전환하여 유연한 시제 운용 능력을 보여줍니다.
✨ 디스코스 마커 및 시간 순서 연결어: 'First off', 'Once', 'As soon as', 'Most of the time', 'looking back' 등의 연결어를 자연스럽게 배치하여 단계별 준비 과정을 논리적이고 유기적으로 전달합니다.
✨ 구체적 감정 표현 및 생생한 묘사: 단순히 짐을 쌌다는 사실 전달에 그치지 않고 'a heart-stopping moment', 'gives me a huge sense of satisfaction' 등 구체적인 감정 상태를 표현하여 원어민다운 구어체 풍부함을 연출했습니다.
