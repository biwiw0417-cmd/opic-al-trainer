2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

You know, looking back, I’ve had my fair share of travel mishaps, but one that really stands out happened last summer. I had booked a boutique hotel in Jeju and was really looking forward to it. The thing is, when I arrived after a long flight, the receptionist told me they were fully booked and had no record of my stay. It was a total unforeseen mix-up.

I was exhausted and didn't want any last-minute hassle, so I stayed calm and showed them my confirmation email on my phone. Even then, they insisted there was nothing in the system. I had to stand my ground because I knew I had paid in full and had the receipt. After about 30 minutes of searching and making phone calls, they realized there was a clerical error on their end—they had entered my name incorrectly.

The manager was extremely apologetic and tried to rectify the situation immediately. Since they actually were out of standard rooms due to the high season, they upgraded me to a suite for free and even provided a voucher for their rooftop bar.

Looking back, it was a stressful start, but it ended up being a blessing in disguise because of the room upgrade. Now, I always make sure to double-check the details and even call the property a few days before I arrive just to be safe.
