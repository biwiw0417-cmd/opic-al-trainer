You indicated in the survey that you enjoy traveling abroad. Tell me about a memorable trip you took overseas. Where did you go, who did you go with, and what made that trip so memorable?

2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Honestly, I’ve been to a few countries, but my solo trip to Japan last summer really stands out. I wanted to step out of my comfort zone and broaden my horizons, so I just bought a plane ticket on an impulse.

When I first arrived in Tokyo, I suffered from severe jet lag, which threw me off a bit. On top of that, I faced a bit of a language barrier because I couldn't speak fluent Japanese. In the past, I used to rely entirely on guided tour groups whenever I traveled abroad. But this time around, I decided to handle everything myself.

To overcome the communication issues, I used translation apps while trying to sample local food at small neighborhood eateries. The food was so incredible that I ended up visiting three different ramen spots in a single day! During the daytime, I would just wander around quiet backstreets, getting lost on purpose. I was able to immerse myself in the local culture and lifestyle in a way I never had before.

Looking back, navigating a foreign country alone gave me so much confidence. Nowadays, whenever work gets overwhelming, I think about that trip and feel ready to tackle any challenge. It was truly an unforgettable journey that changed how I view the world.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(도쿄 나홀로 여행 소개 및 계기) → MP(과거 패키지여행 의존에서 벗어나 독자적으로 도전) → 디테일 확장(시차 적응과 언어 장벽 극복, 현지 음식 탐방 및 골목 배회) → 감정/생각(자유로움과 자신감 획득) → 마무리(현재 일상에 활력을 주는 계기 언급)

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 과거 시제 및 과거 vs 현재 대비: 'used to rely'와 'decided to handle'을 통해 예전 패키지 여행 방식과 이번 자유 여행을 자연스럽게 비교하며 시제 전환 능력을 보여줍니다.
✨ 풍부한 구어체 디스코스 마커 활용: 'Honestly', 'On top of that', 'this time around', 'Looking back' 등을 적재적소에 배치하여 답변의 흐름을 매우 매끄럽게 이어갑니다.
✨ 구체적이고 다채로운 어휘 사용: 'broaden my horizons', 'jet lag', 'language barrier', 'wander around', 'immerse myself in' 등 수준 높은 덩어리 표현을 활용해 구체적인 경험 상황을 채점자에게 전달합니다.
