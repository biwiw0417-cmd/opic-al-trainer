2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Actually, when it comes to traditional holidays in Korea, the biggest one has to be Chuseok, which is often called Korean Thanksgiving. It's a massive red-letter day where the entire country seems to be on the move.

Basically, the core of Chuseok is all about family. People travel from all over to their hometowns, despite the crazy holiday rush, just to gather around with their relatives. The thing is, we have this long-standing tradition of performing ancestral rites. We set up a huge table with various dishes to pay our respects to our ancestors.

But for me, the absolute best part is the homemade feast. My mother and aunts spend all day preparing traditional food like Songpyeon, which are rice cakes. There’s such a festive atmosphere in the kitchen with everyone talking and laughing at the same time.

Looking back, I used to think it was a bit exhausting to travel so far, but now I realize it's the perfect chance to catch up with family and have some quality time. In our busy lives, we don't get many opportunities to just sit down and talk for hours.

So yeah, even though the traffic is a nightmare, the warmth of the family gathering makes it all worth it in the end.

✨ 'Red-letter day', 'Holiday rush'와 같은 고득점 어휘를 사용하여 주제 전문성을 높였습니다.
