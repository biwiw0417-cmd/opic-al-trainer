1오늘의 질문

Sometimes plans change unexpectedly. Tell me about a time when an appointment or a plan you made with someone fell through. What was the situation, and how did you handle it?

🇰🇷 한국어 해석 보기

가끔은 계획이 예기치 않게 변경되기도 하죠. 누군가와 했던 약속이나 계획이 수틀어졌던 경험에 대해 말해주세요. 어떤 상황이었고, 어떻게 대처했나요?

💡 콤보 맥락: 약속(Appointments) 주제의 콤보 질문 중 세 번째인 '과거 경험' 유형입니다. 단순히 일정을 잡는 것을 넘어, 변수가 발생했을 때의 대처와 감정 변화를 과거 시제로 서술하는 능력을 평가합니다.

먼저 스스로 답해보세요! 모범답변을 보기 전에 1분 30초 동안 녹음해 보세요.

2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Actually, I’m the type of person who really tries to keep my word, but I remember this one time a few months ago when things just didn’t go as planned. I had this big social commitment with one of my best friends. We had tickets for a jazz concert that we’d been talking about for ages.

I was really looking forward to it, but just a few hours before the show, my friend called me sounding super stressed. It turns out he had an unexpected conflict at work and was completely double-booked. He felt terrible about it, but he simply couldn't make it. At first, I was a bit bummed out because I’d already cleared my schedule, and it’s not every day you get to see a world-class jazz band.

The thing is, I didn't want the tickets to go to waste. I tried to squeeze someone else in, but it was so last minute that everyone else was busy. Eventually, my friend suggested we take a rain check and promised to treat me to dinner next time.

Looking back, I realized that as much as I value being punctual and reliable, life happens. We ended up rescheduling for the following month. Even though we missed the original concert, we had a great time catching up over a meal later on. It taught me to be a bit more flexible when plans fall through.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론: 약속을 중시하는 성격 언급 및 특정 사건 도입 (Actually, I’m the type of person...) MP: 재즈 콘서트 약속이 취소된 상황 (We had tickets for a jazz concert...) 디테일: 친구의 업무상 겹침(double-booked) 및 나의 아쉬움 (unexpected conflict... bummed out) 감정/대처: 다른 사람을 찾으려 했으나 실패하고 다음을 기약함 (squeeze someone in... rain check) 마무리: 인생에는 변수가 있음을 인정하며 유연함의 중요성 언급 (Looking back... be more flexible)

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 'I’m the type of person who...'와 같은 숙어적 표현으로 서두를 시작하여 화자의 성향을 효과적으로 드러냈습니다.
✨ 과거 진행형(we’d been talking about), 과거 완료(I’d already cleared), 조건절 등 다양한 시제와 문법 구조를 활용해 AL 수준의 유창성을 보여줍니다.
✨ 'bummed out', 'last minute', 'take a rain check' 등 원어민들이 일상적으로 사용하는 구어적 표현이 자연스럽게 배치되었습니다.
