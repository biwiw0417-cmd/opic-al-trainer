🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

To be honest, the housing issue is probably one of the most talked-about topics in Korea right now. The thing is, the cost of living in major cities like Seoul is becoming a serious concern for many. It’s not just a minor inconvenience; it’s a real crisis for the younger generation.

Basically, housing prices have been skyrocketing over the past few years. Even if you work a stable job and save up, buying an apartment in a decent area feels almost out of reach. This has led to a situation where many young adults grapple with high monthly rents, which makes it nearly impossible to save for the future. Looking back, it wasn't this extreme a decade ago, but now there's this huge real estate bubble that hasn't really popped yet.

In my opinion, this housing shortage and the high prices are discouraging people from getting married or having children. It's a domino effect. I feel quite frustrated when I see my friends struggling just to find a tiny studio that they can afford.

I think it’s high time for policy-makers to step up and create more practical solutions. They should prioritize increasing the supply of public housing and stabilizing the rental market. Without these changes, the gap between the rich and the poor will only get wider. So yeah, that’s my take on the current housing situation in Korea.

✨ 과거와 현재의 상황을 비교(a decade ago vs now)하고, 개인의 의견에서 사회적 영향(domino effect)으로 시야를 확장하는 디테일 전개 방식이 돋보입니다.
