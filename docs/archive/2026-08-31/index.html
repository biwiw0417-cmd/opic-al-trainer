1오늘의 질문

Have you ever had to cancel or reschedule an appointment due to an unexpected situation? What was the situation and how did you resolve it?

🇰🇷 한국어 해석 보기

예상치 못한 상황 때문에 약속을 취소하거나 변경해야 했던 적이 있나요? 어떤 상황이었고 어떻게 해결했는지 말씀해 주세요.

💡 콤보 맥락: 롤플레이 11번(질문하기), 12번(대안 제시하며 해결하기)과 이어지는 콤보의 마지막 질문으로, 실제 본인의 유사한 경험을 설명해야 하는 유형입니다.

먼저 스스로 답해보세요! 모범답변을 보기 전에 1분 30초 동안 녹음해 보세요.

2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Well, actually, I remember this one time a few years ago when I had to cancel on my best friend for a concert we'd been looking forward to for months. It was a total unexpected turn of events.

The thing is, my younger brother suddenly got into a minor car accident on the day of the show. He wasn't badly hurt, but since my parents were out of town, I was the only one who could help him. Obviously, I had to prioritize my family, but I felt so guilty about the last-minute change, especially since the tickets were quite expensive.

I called my friend right away and offered a sincere apology. I explained that it was an unavoidable circumstance and that I felt terrible about flaking on her. Looking back, I was really worried she’d be upset, but she was incredibly understanding.

To smooth things over, I promised to treat her to a fancy dinner and pay for the next concert tickets. Even though I couldn't go that night, I made sure to rectify the situation later. It taught me that when an unforeseen conflict arises, being honest and direct is the best way to maintain a friendship.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(친구와의 콘서트 약속 회상) → MP(동생의 사고로 인한 갑작스러운 상황 발생) → 디테일(가족을 우선순위에 두어야 했던 상황과 죄책감) → 해결(진심 어린 사과와 보상 제안) → 마무리(솔직함의 중요성을 깨달음)

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 과거 시제 서사(got into, was, explained)가 답변 내내 일관되게 유지되어 시제 관리 능력을 증명합니다.
✨ 'unexpected turn of events', 'unavoidable circumstance'와 같은 고득점 어휘를 사용하여 상황을 구체화했습니다.
✨ 'The thing is', 'Looking back'과 같은 디스코스 마커를 적재적소에 배치하여 답변의 흐름이 자연스러운 원어민 구어체처럼 느껴지게 합니다.

4오늘의 패턴 2

I felt so [adjective] about [noun/gerund], especially since [clause].
~에 대해 정말 [형용사]하게 느꼈어요, 특히 ~해서 더 그랬죠.
오늘 답변I felt so guilty about the last-minute change, especially since the tickets were quite expensive.
응용I felt so anxious about the presentation, especially since my boss was watching me.
Looking back, it taught me how to [verb]...
돌이켜보니, 그것은 저에게 ~하는 법을 가르쳐 주었습니다.
오늘 답변Looking back, it taught me how to handle these awkward situations with honesty.
응용Looking back, it taught me how to manage my time more efficiently.
