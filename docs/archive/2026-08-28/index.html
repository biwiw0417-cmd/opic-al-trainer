2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Hey Sarah, it's me. Listen, I have some bad news regarding our plans for the concert tonight. I’m so sorry to tell you this, but I’m afraid I won’t be able to make it.

Something urgent has come up at work quite suddenly. My manager just assigned me a last-minute report that needs to be finished by tomorrow morning, so I’m completely tied up until late tonight. I tried to push it off, but leaving the office right now is simply out of the question.

I feel terrible about this, so I’ve thought of a few ways to fix this. First, why don't you reach out to Chloe? I know she really wanted to see this band, and I can transfer my ticket to her phone right away so you don't have to go alone.

If you'd rather not go without me, we could try to find tickets for their second show this Sunday instead. I'll cover the full cost of the new tickets to compensate for this mess. Also, I’d love to treat you to a nice dinner tomorrow to make up for flaking on you today.

Again, I’m incredibly sorry for the last-minute change. Let me know what works best for you. I’ll be waiting for your text!

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(인사 및 나쁜 소식 예고) → MP(업무상 급한 일로 못 가게 됨) → 디테일(상사 업무 지시로 퇴근 불가능 강조) → 대안 제시(다른 친구와 가기, 다음 공연 예매 및 비용 부담, 저녁 식사 대접) → 마무리(사과 및 답변 요청)

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ I'm afraid I won't be able to...와 같은 격식 있고 자연스러운 거절 표현을 사용하여 실제 원어민의 전화 에티켓을 잘 보여주었습니다.
✨ Out of the question과 같은 강한 부정 표현을 활용하여 상황의 불가피성을 효과적으로 전달하며 어휘력을 증명했습니다.
✨ 상대방의 기분을 고려한 세 가지 구체적인 대안(티켓 양도, 재예매, 보상 식사)을 제시하여 논리적인 문제 해결 능력을 보여주었습니다.

4오늘의 패턴 2

I'm afraid I won't be able to [verb]...
유감스럽게도 ~하지 못할 것 같습니다.
오늘 답변I’m afraid I won’t be able to make it.
응용I'm afraid I won't be able to attend the meeting tomorrow morning.
If you'd rather not [verb], we could always [verb]...
만약 당신이 ~하는 걸 원치 않으신다면, 언제든 ~할 수도 있습니다.
오늘 답변If you'd rather not go without me, we could try to find tickets for their second show.
응용If you'd rather not cook tonight, we could always order some pizza.
