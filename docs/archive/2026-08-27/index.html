2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

Hi there, I’m calling to **inquire about** the Tokyo tour package I saw on your website earlier today. I’m planning a trip for next month and had a few things I wanted to double-check before booking.

First of all, I was wondering about the **availability** for the third week of September. I know that can be a bit of a **peak season**, so I wanted to make sure there are still spots left for a group of four. Also, what is the exact **duration** of the tour? The website said five days, but I wasn't sure if that included travel days.

Secondly, regarding the price, is the package truly **all-inclusive**? I’d like to know if it covers all meals and **lodging**, or if there are any **additional fees** I should be prepared for, like service tips or entrance fees for certain landmarks.

Also, since there are four of us traveling together, do you happen to offer a **group discount**? It would be great if we could save a little bit on the total cost. Could you **get a quote** for us and email it to my address?

Oh, and one more thing—could you provide more details on the **sightseeing itinerary**? We’re really looking for a mix of modern and traditional spots. Anyway, I’d appreciate it if you could get back to me as soon as possible. Thanks!

🧭 답변 뼈대
[인사 및 전화 목적: inquire about 활용] → [가용성 및 기간 확인: availability, peak season, duration 활용] → [포함 내역 및 비용 상세 질문: all-inclusive, lodging, additional fees 활용] → [할인 요청 및 견적 문의: group discount, get a quote 활용] → [일정 상세 요청 및 마무리: sightseeing itinerary 활용]

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ I was wondering about...이나 Do you happen to offer...와 같은 간접 의문문을 사용하여 격식 있는 롤플레이 톤을 완벽하게 유지했습니다.
✨ 단순한 질문을 넘어 'spots left for a group of four'나 'mix of modern and traditional spots'와 같은 구체적인 디테일을 추가해 답변의 풍부함을 더했습니다.
✨ Anyway, I'd appreciate it if...와 같은 자연스러운 마무리 문구를 사용하여 실제 상황 같은 유창함을 보여주었습니다.
