I'd like to know about your interest in fashion. What kind of clothes do you usually like to wear for different occasions? Tell me about your personal style in detail.

2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

To be honest, I wouldn't say I'm some kind of fashion-forward trendsetter, but I do take pride in my fashion sense. When it comes to my personal style, I'm a big fan of the "effortless look."

My go-to outfit for daily life is definitely smart-casual. You know, something like a well-fitted blazer with clean denim. I love to mix and match different textures because it keeps things interesting without being too loud. I feel like wardrobe staples like a crisp white shirt or a quality leather jacket are the real game-changers because they are so versatile.

However, when I need to dress for the occasion, like a wedding or a job interview, I always lean towards timeless classics. I think it's important to look the part and show respect to the host. I don't really try to stand out with flashy colors. Instead, I prefer understated elegance that makes me feel sophisticated.

The thing is, I've realized that no matter what you wear, the key is how you pull off the look with confidence. Looking back, I used to just follow trends blindly, but now I know what actually suits my body type. It’s quite satisfying to have a style that truly represents who I am.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(패션에 대한 태도) → MP(평소 선호하는 스마트 캐주얼 스타일) → 디테일(아이템 조합 및 필수 아이템 설명) → 상황별 스타일(격식 있는 자리에서의 클래식 스타일) → 감정 및 과거 비교(자신감의 중요성과 스타일 변화) → 마무리

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 'smart-casual', 'mix and match' 등 전문적인 패션 어휘를 적재적소에 배치하여 어휘의 범위를 증명했습니다.
✨ 'Looking back'을 사용하여 과거의 맹목적인 트렌드 추종과 현재의 주관 있는 스타일을 대조하며 시제 유연성을 보여주었습니다.
✨ 'The thing is', 'To be honest'와 같은 디스코스 마커를 사용하여 문장 간의 연결성을 자연스럽게 확보했습니다.
