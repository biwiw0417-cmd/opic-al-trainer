2AL 모범답변 + 전략 해설

🔒 스스로 답한 후 열어보세요 — 탭해서 열기

When it comes to domestic travel, I have a pretty set routine that helps me make the most of my time. Honestly, the prep work is just as exciting as the trip itself.

I usually start by browsing through social media to find some hidden gems that aren't too crowded. Once I pick a destination, I book a stay right away to get the best deals. A few days before leaving, I double-check the itinerary to make sure everything is in order. The most important rule for me is to pack light; I've realized that dragging a heavy suitcase only slows me down.

On the day of the trip, I try to hit the road as early as possible to beat the traffic. Once I arrive, the first thing I do is check into my accommodation and then head out to find some local delicacies. I'm a huge foodie, so trying regional specialties is a must.

I spend the rest of the day wandering around and snapping photos of any scenic view I come across. I'm not a fan of tight schedules, so I prefer to just soak up the atmosphere at my own pace. Looking back, having this flexible yet organized routine is what makes my domestic trips so refreshing and stress-free.

🧭 답변 뼈대
서론(국내 여행 루틴의 즐거움 언급) → MP(준비 과정: 숨은 명소 찾기, 숙소 예약, 일정 재확인, 가볍게 짐 싸기) → 디테일(여행 당일: 일찍 출발하기, 지역 맛집 탐방, 사진 찍기) → 감정/마무리(유연한 루틴이 주는 스트레스 해소 효과 강조)

이 답변이 AL인 이유 3가지

✨ 'make the most of my time'이나 'as exciting as the trip itself'와 같은 자연스러운 원어민 표현을 서두에 배치하여 유창함을 보여주었습니다.
✨ 준비 과정(pack light, double-check)에서 실제 여행지에서의 행동(hit the road, check into)으로 이어지는 시제 흐름과 논리적 연결이 매우 매끄럽습니다.
✨ 'Looking back'이라는 디스코스 마커를 사용하여 답변을 요약하고 본인의 느낌을 정리하며 마무리하는 AL의 전형적인 구조를 갖췄습니다.
